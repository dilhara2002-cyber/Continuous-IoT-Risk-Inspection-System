Device Classification

OUI manufacturer resolution and rule-based heuristic categorization

Two-Stage Classification Workflow:
1. Stage 1 (Manufacturer Identification): Extracts first 3 octets (MAC OUI) and queries against the IEEE Vendor database.
2. Stage 2 (Heuristic Inference): Inspects open port profiles (e.g. RTSP 554, JetDirect 9100) and service banners to infer the device category or fallback to Unknown Device.
