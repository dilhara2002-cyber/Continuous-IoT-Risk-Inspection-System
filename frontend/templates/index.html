Device Classification

All Discovered Devices — OUI manufacturer resolution and rule-based heuristic categorization

Two-Stage Classification Workflow:
1. Stage 1 (Manufacturer Identification): Extracts first 3 octets (MAC OUI) and queries against the IEEE Vendor database. If unlisted in the local IEEE registry, vendor data originating from stored device metadata (network scan / testbed) is distinguished and explicitly labeled.
2. Stage 2 (Heuristic Inference): Inspects open port profiles (e.g. RTSP 554, JetDirect 9100), service banners, and hostname signatures to infer the device category or fallback to Unknown Device.
